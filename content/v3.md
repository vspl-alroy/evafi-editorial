## How The Minimum Payment Trap Actually Works

Card issuers calculate your minimum payment as a small percentage of your balance — commonly 1% to 3% — plus interest, or a flat minimum dollar amount, whichever is higher. It's designed to look affordable every month, not to shrink your balance, and on a large enough balance it can drag repayment out for a decade or more.

Here's what that meant for Allison's $11,596 balance: paid off using minimum payments alone, it would have cost $37,661 — more than three times what was originally owed.

## The Paths People Use To Get Out

- Consolidation loans roll multiple debts into a single loan, often at a lower rate and one simpler payment.
- Balance transfer cards move debt to a low- or 0%-interest card, so payments chip away at principal instead of interest.
- Debt settlement programs negotiate with creditors to resolve debt for less than the full balance — often used for larger or long-standing balances.

Programs like these are built specifically for people in the minimum payment trap, not just people who are a little behind.
