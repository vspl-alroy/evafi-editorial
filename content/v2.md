## The Minimum Payment Math Nobody Explains

When you get a credit card statement, the minimum due looks small and manageable. That's the point. Issuers typically calculate it as a small percentage of your balance — often just 1% to 3% — plus interest, or a flat dollar floor, whichever is bigger. On a large enough balance, that structure can stretch repayment out for a decade or more.

## What Getting Out Can Actually Look Like

Once debt has built up, there are a few well-known routes out, depending on the situation:

- Consolidation loans combine several balances into one loan and one payment, usually at a lower rate than credit cards charge.
- Balance transfer cards shift debt to a card with a 0% or low promotional rate, buying time to pay down principal instead of interest.
- Debt settlement programs work directly with creditors to settle debts for less than what's owed, which can be the fastest way out for larger balances.

Programs like these are built specifically for people in the minimum payment trap, not just people who are a little behind.
